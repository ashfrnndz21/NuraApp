Why

Each recommendation names the rule it came from, the evidence, and what happens if it's left. The clinic decides urgency; the planner only asks.

  • Results before reviews: a test is sequenced so the doctor who needs it has it.
  • Medicine changes set their own monitoring: a diuretic change means electrolytes and kidney function within two weeks.
  • Conditions carry cadences: eyes and feet yearly with diabetes, lipids yearly, sugar every three to six months.
  • Feelings adjust urgency, not specialty, unless they match a red flag.
  • Same hospital, same day, where the calendar allows. Family logistics and panel status are constraints, not afterthoughts.
  • Nothing is booked without a confirm; red flags bypass the plan and go to the same-day path.
