# Nura — brand guide, v1

The mark is the open heart of two halves. It is fixed; everything else in the product uses it as-is.

---

## 1. The mark

Two strokes that meet at the top and never close at the bottom. The left half is plum; the right half is the aura gradient. The dot inside is the person. Two people, the patient and the one who cares for him, with an opening because the care is never finished.

Read it at any size as: a heart, made of two people, with one person held inside.

## 2. Files

`nura-brand/`
- `nura-mark.svg` — the mark, two-colour. Use on light backgrounds.
- `nura-mark-mono.svg` — one colour, takes `currentColor`. Use on photos with a paper surface behind it, on dark backgrounds, in print.
- `nura-lockup.svg` — mark plus the wordmark "nura" in Outfit Medium. Use for the App Store listing, splash, documents.
- `nura-icon-wash.svg` — app icon on the dawn wash. The App Store icon and the default.
- `nura-icon-plum.svg` — app icon on plum. Dark mode and the Lock Screen.
- `nura-avatar-whatsapp.svg` — the speaking heart on blush, for the WhatsApp business profile and notification avatar.
- `png/` — icon at 1024, 512, 180, 167, 152, 120, 87, 80, 76, 60, 58, 40, 29; plum icon at 1024, 180, 120; mark at 512 to 16; lockup at 1040 wide; avatar at 512.

## 3. Colour

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Plum, the left stroke and the dot | `#4E3A78` |
| Aura start, the right stroke | `#B9A6E0` |
| Aura end | `#F0C9DA` |
| Wash, icon background | `#DCD5EE` → `#F1DCE6` → `#DCE8DF` at 160° |
| Paper, the surface the mark sits on in the app | white at 92% |

On plum: the left stroke and the dot become white; the right stroke keeps the aura. Never invert the aura onto a light background.

## 4. Space and size

Clear space around the mark equals the diameter of the dot on every side. Minimum size: 16 px for the mark, 80 px wide for the lockup. Below 24 px use the mono mark.

## 5. Wordmark

"nura", lowercase, Outfit Medium, tracking −2%. Uppercase only in the App Store title if the platform forces it. The wordmark never sits without the mark on first use in any surface; after that, the wordmark can stand alone in running text.

## 6. Do and don't

Do: use the two-colour mark on paper and light washes; use white-and-aura on plum; keep the opening at the bottom; keep the dot.
Don't: close the heart; fill it; add a pulse line, a cross or a stethoscope; rotate it; put it on a photo without the paper surface; recolour the halves; stretch the wordmark.

## 7. Motion

Two states only. Idle: nothing. Speaking: the right half's aura gradient drifts slowly through its two colours over four seconds, and the dot breathes by 6%. Used when Nura plays a voice note or a clip. Respects Reduce Motion by falling back to a static opacity change.

## 8. Voice name

The assistant is Nura. It signs WhatsApp messages "Nura". It never says "the app" or "the system". The mark is the assistant's face: the WhatsApp avatar, the notification icon and the speaker button in the app all use it.
