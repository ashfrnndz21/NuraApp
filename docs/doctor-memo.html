After: every question closed with the answer at its timestamp, changes as proposals, a new memo in his words, and the reminders that come out of it.

How the memo is built. "Since last time" is the State diff between the last visit and today: readings, feeling taps, results, adherence, weight, and anything the family reported. Questions come from six sources, each shown: results, feeling taps, readings, family reports, the polypharmacy review, and gaps in the record; they are ranked by what changed most, and every question has a clinician wording and a patient wording. "Worth knowing" comes from the self-search jobs, allowlisted sources only, checked against his actual medicines and packs, and always phrased as a question for the doctor. After the visit, each question is closed with the sentence from the recording at its timestamp; unanswered ones carry forward; changes are proposals until confirmed or a label arrives; and the new memo in his words becomes what the nudges quote until the next visit.
