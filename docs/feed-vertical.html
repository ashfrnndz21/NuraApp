The feed, one screen at a time

TikTok's shape, without TikTok's appetite. One card fills the screen. Swipe up for the next. Each one speaks when it arrives. Today's cards come first; after them, a gentle gate; after that, as much as he wants, all still about him.

Swipe up for the next one

How this stays right for him

The interaction is the simplest one on any phone: one thing, then swipe up. That's why it suits an older person better than a list. What changes is the supply.

Order of supply

  • Now — the tablet, the reading, the visit. Always first.
  • Today — one or two cards made for him today, from his own data and allowed sources.
  • The gate — "That is all that is new. Keep going?" One tap to continue.
  • His own story — recall cards: what Dr Tan said, how his sugar has changed, the walk on Sunday, the grandkids at the park.
  • Learning — short explainers about his conditions and tablets, from allowed sources, in his language. Endless, evergreen, never news.

What it never does

  • Autoplay the next card. Each one waits for him.
  • Show anything not about him, or from a source not on the list.
  • Count, badge, or reward. No "you watched 12".
  • Put a doctor question or a safety notice in front of him. Those go to Ash.
  • Optimise for time in the feed. Opens of the tablet card matter; minutes do not.

For Ash and for people managing their own health

The same vertical feed with no gate, denser cards, sources on every one, and the doctor questions inline. Genuinely endless, because their appetite is different.
