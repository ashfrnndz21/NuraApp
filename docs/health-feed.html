Sources allowed
Health authorities (MOH, HSA, NPRA), hospital groups (Gleneagles, Sunway, KPJ, SingHealth), professional bodies (heart, kidney, diabetes societies), and their video channels. No forums, no supplement pages, no news sites.
The rule that keeps it small. A card reaches Pa only if it is about him, from an allowed source, rewritten in plain words, and one of at most two non-reminder cards that day. Anything that is really a question for the doctor goes to Ash and the Doctor Memo, not to Pa. Anything with nothing to do is held. The feed for Pa says "That is all for today" and means it.
