Nura — logo concepts

Four original marks in the app's own tone: lavender, blush and sage washes, plum as the single accent, Outfit for the wordmark. Each is shown as the mark alone, the lockup, and the app icon on the wash and on plum. None borrows a heart, a pulse line or a cross.

1. Shelter and cradle recommended

The first two letters of the name, stacked. The "n" is an arch: a roof, shelter, someone standing over you. The "u" is a bowl: a cradle, holding. The dot is the person. Read together it is the "nu" of Nura; read as a picture it is care around a person. It scales to a favicon and survives being cut into a Lock Screen glyph.

2. Halo n

The "n" alone, drawn as one soft stroke, with a ring of light resting on its shoulder. The aura made literal, but quiet. Works well where the wordmark is set beside it; the ring picks up the wash colours and the stroke stays plum.

3. Aura rings

A person, and three soft arcs around them that fade outward: the aura, and the family and system around one man. The arcs open upward like a cradle. It is the most abstract of the four and the most at home on a dark Lock Screen.

4. Dawn

The morning card is Nura's main moment, and the app's background is a dawn wash. So: a sun at the horizon with two thin rings of light above it. Warm, calm, and it doubles as the "stable" state glyph inside the app.

Recommendation. Concept 1, shelter and cradle. It is built from the name, it means what the product does, it has no medical clichés, it works in one colour, and it is the only one of the four that still reads at 16 pixels. Use the halo ring from concept 2 as the "Nura is speaking" animation in the app, and the dawn from concept 4 as the stable-state glyph, so the family of shapes stays consistent.
  • Mark in plum on light backgrounds; white on plum; never on a photo without the paper surface behind it.
  • The cradle stroke takes the aura gradient; the arch and the dot stay solid so the mark keeps its weight.
  • Clear space equal to the dot's diameter on all sides. Minimum size 16 px for the mark, 80 px for the lockup.
  • App icon: the mark on the wash for the App Store; the plum variant for dark mode and the Lock Screen.
  • Separate SVG files are provided for the recommended mark, the lockup and the icon.
