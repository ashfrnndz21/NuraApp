What this prototype covers

Plain words on every screen Pa sees: purpose first, medicine name small and second, numbers explained in a sentence. The same record is shown to Ash with names and sources, and to the helper as a WhatsApp list in her language.

Pa

  • Today — the feed. Ask bar (tap), camera (add), Now card with Taken, explainer clips (tap to play with captions), visit card, food snap, family note, local alert, weekly clip, not-feeling-well.
  • Medicines — purpose-first cards, Taken decrements the count, reorder actions, what-is-this-pill.
  • Records — Add flow (photo, screenshots, PDF, WhatsApp, record a visit) → organising → review with confidence underlines → filed, feed and questions update. Four ways to browse. Emergency card.
  • Visits — next visit with logistics, insurance letter status, questions (expand, hear them), last visit with clip, what he agreed in his words.
  • Not feeling well — three steps: what's wrong, checking his readings and tablets, what to do now, family told.

Ash

  • Home — ask-or-find (records, web, doctors, videos), most likely state with drivers, what changed, next visit, reorder, gaps, what the system is watching, what Pa opened.
  • Timeline — filter by body system.
  • Medicines — week of confirmations, reconciled list with sources, medication story, draft reorder, things to raise on Thursday (talking points, never dose changes).
  • Plan — doctor's page and second-opinion packet, insurance letter tracker, costs, routine toggles, who does what, send Pa something with live preview in his format.
  • Family — roles and scopes, helper settings, family thread, who looked at what.

Helper

  • WhatsApp only, in Bahasa Indonesia: today's list with pill descriptions, "given" replies, "he is not well" button, Thursday's reminder.

Not in this file

  • Real photography or illustration in place of the gradient scenes; real voice; Hokkien, Malay, Mandarin text; onboarding health biography; care navigation results; clip generation.
