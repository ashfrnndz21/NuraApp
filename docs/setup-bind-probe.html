The app starts with the patient. Setup configures his context once, and everything he sees is rendered from it. Family members are bound to that context with scoped grants and his consent. A bound person can then ask about him, and gets answers only from the parts they were given.

How the three panes connect. Setup writes Pa's context once: language, how he reads, conditions, medicines, doctors, insurance, routine, his own words. That context is the State every card, voice note and clip is rendered from. Binding does not share the account; it cuts a key per person with a role, a scope, a time window and Pa's recorded consent, and it leaves an audit trail he can read. Probing runs a bound person's question over Pa's memory, answers with provenance, and refuses politely and visibly when the question falls outside the key. The same three mechanisms serve a spouse, a sibling, a helper, and later a clinic.
